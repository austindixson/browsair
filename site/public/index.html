What's inside

Everything a browser should do. Nothing it shouldn't.

A real WebKit page surface wrapped in native SwiftUI chrome — no Electron, no telemetry, no bloat.

Native WebKit engine

Safari's own rendering and JavaScript — the same performance and compatibility, in a window you control.

Tabs, back & forward, reload

Real tabs with per-tab history, live address updates, and keyboard-first navigation.

Downloads

Save files straight from a page, with a native progress surface and default locations.

Privacy-first storage

Cookies and site data persist locally on your Mac. No accounts, no analytics, nothing phoned home.

History & bookmarks

Browse history and your saved pages, stored locally and available the moment you open a tab.

AI on demand

An optional sidebar for "explain this page" and "find on page" — invoked by you, never reading in the background.

Private by design

Your browsing stays on your Mac.

Browsair has no servers of its own and no usage analytics. Site data lives in your local WebKit store and the macOS Keychain — never on our end.

  • No account, no sign-in.Download, open, and browse. We never ask who you are.
  • Local-only storage.Cookies, localStorage and history persist on-device, exactly where you'd expect.
  • Passkeys are on the way.We've requested Apple's browser passkeys entitlement, so iCloud Keychain passkeys will sign you in right inside Browsair.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

One drag, then trust it the first time — the standard path for any app you didn't get from the App Store.

1 · Requirements
macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.
2 · Download
Grab the .dmg — Browsair is Developer ID signed, and open source.
3 · Install
Open the disk image and drag Browsair into your Applications folder.
4 · First launch
If Gatekeeper raises a warning, right-click (or Control-click) Browsair in Applications and choose Open, then confirm. After that it opens normally.
